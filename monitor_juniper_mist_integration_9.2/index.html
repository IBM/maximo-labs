Welcome to the Maximo Monitor 9.2
Juniper Mist Integration Lab

Info

This hands-on lab demonstrates how to integrate Juniper Mist with IBM Maximo Monitor 9.2.4 & 9.3 FC.

In this lab, you will explore Juniper Mist and learn how to set up its integration with Maximo Monitor. You will perform step-by-step exercises to configure Juniper Mist, synchronize site hierarchies, assign Maximo Manage locations, configure Business Unit mappings, and subscribe locations to ingest real-time events.

By the end of the lab, you will understand how Wi-Fi telemetry and location intelligence from Juniper Mist are transformed into actionable real-time occupancy and presence analytics across sites, buildings, floors, and business units to drive workplace optimization, capacity planning, and facilities operations.

Lab Exercises

You will complete the following exercises:

  • Juniper Mist Overview
  • Configure Juniper Mist Integration
  • Edit Juniper Mist Integration
  • Site Synchronization Overview
  • Assign Maximo Manage Locations
  • Subscribe Juniper Mist Locations
  • Access Points & Device Types
  • Occupancy & Presence Calculated Metrics
  • Unsubscribe & Unassign Workflows
  • Delete Juniper Mist Integration

Note

Expected time needed to run the complete lab: 1 hour
